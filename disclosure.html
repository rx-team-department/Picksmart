Effective Date: January 1, 2025

Full Transparency: PickSmart.pk earns affiliate commissions from Daraz and other programs at no extra cost to you. This disclosure is provided in compliance with FTC guidelines and Google AdSense policies.

1. What Are Affiliate Links?

Affiliate links are special URLs that track when you click through from our Website to a merchant's website (such as Daraz.pk) and make a purchase. When you buy something using our affiliate link, the merchant pays us a commission. This commission is at no additional cost to you—you pay the same price whether you use our link or not.

2. Our Affiliate Programs

PickSmart.pk currently participates in the following affiliate programs:

  • Daraz Affiliate Program: We earn commissions on products purchased through our Daraz links.

Affiliate commissions help us:

4. Our Commitment to Honesty

Despite earning affiliate commissions, we are committed to providing honest, unbiased reviews and recommendations. Here's how we ensure integrity:

  • Independent Testing: We test products independently based on real-world performance.
  • User Feedback: We consider feedback from our readers and the community.
