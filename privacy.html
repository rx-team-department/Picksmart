Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Introduction

PickSmart.pk ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website at picksmart.pk (the "Website").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Contact Information: When you submit a contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message.
  • Comments: When you post a comment on an article, we collect your name, comment text, and timestamp.
  • Newsletter Signup: We collect your email address if you subscribe to our newsletter.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies may store your language preference and consent status.
  • Google Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our Website. Google Analytics collects information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the Website.
  • Firebase Analytics: We use Firebase Analytics to track user engagement, page views, and article interactions.
  • Log Data: Our servers automatically record information created by your access to the Website, including IP address, browser type, and pages viewed.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To publish and moderate comments on our articles
  • To send you newsletters and promotional content (only if you opt-in)
  • To analyze and improve our Website's performance and user experience
  • To track page views and article engagement
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Firebase

We use Google Firebase to store your comments, article data, and related information in the cloud. Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Your comments and interactions may be stored and processed in multiple locations. By using our Website, you consent to this data storage.

5. Third-Party Services

Our Website uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): For database and storage services
  • Google Analytics: For website analytics
  • Formspree: For contact form submissions

These services may collect information as described in their respective privacy policies.

PickSmart.pk participates in affiliate programs, including the Daraz Affiliate Program. When you click on affiliate links and make a purchase, we may earn a commission. This does not affect the price you pay. Please refer to our Affiliate Disclosure page for more details.

7. Google AdSense

Our Website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may collect information about your browsing habits to serve personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Ad Settings and Privacy & Terms for more information.

8. Cookie Consent

When you first visit our Website, a cookie consent banner appears. By clicking "Accept," you consent to our use of cookies for analytics and user experience enhancement. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including:

To exercise these rights, please contact us at smartdealsproamazon@gmail.com.

We implement security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no internet-based service is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.
